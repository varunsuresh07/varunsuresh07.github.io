// Objectives
  • Encourage creativity and innovation through visual expression.
  • Develop observation skills and the ability to capture meaningful moments.
  • Provide a platform for students to express ideas, emotions, and stories through photography.
  • Enhance technical knowledge of photography — framing, lighting, and composition.
  • Promote artistic thinking and unique perspective among participants.
  • Create a competitive environment that motivates students to showcase their talent.
// Event Structure
Round 1
Theme-Based Photography
🎯
The Theme

A theme will be announced on the spot. Participants must capture photographs based on the given theme within the event duration.

⏱
Time Limit

45–60 minutes. All photos must be taken within the campus premises only. Previously captured images are strictly prohibited.

↓
Round 2
Creative Storytelling Final Round
📸
Photo Submission

Shortlisted participants submit 3–5 photographs based on a given theme. Selection for this round is based on Round 1 performance.

🗣
The Story

Participants must briefly explain the story or concept behind their photographs to the judges. Your narrative matters as much as your lens.

// Rules & Instructions
  • Each participant must use their own device — mobile phone or dedicated camera. Borrowed devices are not permitted.
  • Only basic editing is allowed: brightness, contrast, and cropping. Advanced filters, AI tools, or photo manipulation of any kind is strictly prohibited.
  • All photographs must be captured during the event duration only. Use of previously taken or externally sourced images leads to immediate disqualification.
  • Plagiarism or submission of images not taken by the participant during the event will result in disqualification.
  • Participants must adhere to the given time limit strictly. Late entries will not be accepted under any circumstance.
  • Participants are expected to maintain discipline throughout the event. Any violation of rules will result in disqualification.
  • The decision of the judges is final and binding. No appeals or disputes will be entertained after judging is complete.
