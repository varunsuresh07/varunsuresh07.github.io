// About the Event

The Free Fire battleground is calling! Step into the arena where only the strongest survive. This isn't just a tournament — it's a war of skill, strategy, and nerves. Drop in, gear up, and eliminate your enemies in a ruthless fight for survival.

Experience heart-pounding action, intense battles, and unstoppable competition as players clash to prove who truly dominates the battlefield. Only the sharpest minds and fastest hands will survive the chaos.

This is your moment to rise, conquer, and claim glory. Squad up, stay sharp, and get ready — because only one will emerge as the ultimate survivor. 🎮🔥

// Team & Format
  • Each team consists of 4 players only. No substitutes are permitted under any circumstance.
  • Only Android and iOS mobile devices are allowed for gameplay.
  • Emulators and iPads are strictly prohibited. Violations lead to immediate disqualification.
// Gameplay Rules
  • Any use of game-modifying tools or third-party software will lead to immediate disqualification.
  • In-game voice chat is mandatory for proper team coordination throughout all rounds.
  • Use of unfair means — aimbot, trigger bot, or ESP — results in instant disqualification and ban.
  • Bug exploitation or any act that hinders fair play will lead to disqualification of the entire team.
  • Players must join 15 minutes before match start. Late teams forfeit their slot — no exceptions.
  • In case of a tie, the number of Booyahs (wins) will decide the champion.
