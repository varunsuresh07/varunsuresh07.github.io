// About the Event

The Gaming tournament at PARICHAY 4.0 is the ultimate battleground for competitive players. High-stakes brackets across BGMI, Valorant, and FIFA test reflexes, macro strategy, and team coordination under intense pressure. From 1v1 aim duels to full squad operations, every format is engineered to eliminate casual players and crown the truly elite. Prizes await those with the fastest hands and sharpest game sense.

// Rules & Regulations
  • Participants must bring their own device (mobile/PC/controller). Stable network access will be provided by organizers on-site.
  • All matches played on official tournament servers with settings locked by the event team. No custom configs beyond permitted loadouts.
  • Be present and ready 10 minutes before scheduled match time. Late players forfeit their slot — no exceptions.
  • Hacks, aimbots, exploits, or any 3rd-party performance software result in permanent ban from this and all future PARICHAY editions.
  • Toxic behaviour, harassment, or verbal abuse toward anyone in the venue results in immediate disqualification without refund.
  • Format: Round-robin group stage then single-elimination knockout. Group seeding based on match record and point differential.
  • Disputes raised with the match referee immediately. Post-match complaints not entertained under any circumstance.
