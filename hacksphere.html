// About the Event

Hacksphere is PARICHAY 4.0's flagship 24-hour hackathon — an intense coding marathon where participants form teams to ideate, design, and build tech solutions to real-world problems. From AI-powered web apps to hardware hacks, every domain is welcome. Expert industry mentors guide teams through the night. Projects are judged on innovation, technical depth, design quality, and real-world viability. First-timers and seasoned developers alike compete on equal ground.

// Rules & Regulations
  • Teams must consist of 2 to 4 members. Solo participation is not permitted under any circumstance.
  • All code must be written during the hackathon window. Pre-written codebases or plagiarised work leads to immediate disqualification.
  • Open source libraries and public APIs are allowed. All third-party assets must be properly credited in the submission.
  • Submit a working prototype plus a presentation deck (max 10 slides) before the deadline. Both are mandatory.
  • Judged on: Innovation, Technical Complexity, UI/UX Design, and Real-world Impact. All four criteria weighted equally.
  • All team members must be present during the final demo and Q&A with judges. Absence = disqualification.
  • Organiser decisions on judging, eligibility, and disqualification are final and binding with no appeal.
