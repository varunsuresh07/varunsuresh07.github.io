// About the Event

Hacksphere is PARICHAY 4.0's flagship 24-hour hackathon — a two-part endurance race designed to filter the best ideas before they reach the stage. Not just a build-athon: it's a structured gauntlet that tests your ability to ship fast, adapt under pressure, and pitch with clarity. The theme will be revealed at the start of the event. Every domain is welcome — web, mobile, AI, hardware.

// The Format
Phase 1
Online  Hours 1 – 12
The Build

Teams work from home to build their core prototype. No setup time wasted — get straight to coding.

The Cut

At the 12-hour mark, teams submit their progress. Only the best-performing teams are selected to advance to campus.

→
Phase 2
On-Campus  Hours 13 – 24
The Polish

Selected teams arrive on campus to finalize their project, fix bugs, and sharpen their presentation.

The Pitch

A final live demo in front of a panel of judges. Make it count.

// Key Rules
  • Originality — No pre-built projects. All code must be written within the 24-hour window. Pre-existing codebases = instant disqualification.
  • Team Size — 2 to 4 members per team. Solo entries are not accepted.
  • The Check-In — Teams must submit a 12-hour milestone report (code + demo video) to be eligible for the campus round. No submission = no entry to Phase 2.
  • Attendance — If selected, the entire team must be physically present for Phase 2. Missing members may lead to disqualification of the full team.
  • Tools — Open-source libraries and AI assistants are allowed. However, you must be able to explain every line of your code to the judges.
