// About the Event

Hacksphere is IGNITE's flagship hackathon — a 24-hour intense coding marathon where participants form teams to ideate, design, and build tech solutions addressing real-world problems. From web and mobile apps to AI-driven tools, participants across all domains converge in a creative frenzy. Expert mentors guide teams through the night, and projects are judged on innovation, execution, and impact. Whether you're a seasoned developer or a first-time hacker, Hacksphere is where legends are built.

// Rules & Regulations
  • Teams must consist of 2 to 4 members. Solo participation is not permitted under any circumstance.
  • All code must be written during the hackathon window. Pre-written codebases or plagiarised work results in immediate disqualification.
  • Open source libraries and public APIs are allowed. Any third-party assets used must be properly attributed in the final submission.
  • Teams must submit a working prototype along with a presentation deck (max 10 slides) before the deadline.
  • Projects judged on Innovation, Technical Complexity, UI/UX Design, and Real-world Impact.
  • All team members must be present and available for the final demo and Q&A round with judges.
  • Organiser decisions regarding judging, eligibility, and disqualification are final and binding.
