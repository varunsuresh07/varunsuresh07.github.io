About The Fest

Four editions. One legacy. Infinite potential.

PARICHAY 4.0 is our college's premier annual technology and creative festival by the AIML department— a full-spectrum intellectual and competitive battleground where engineers, coders, designers and dreamers collide to push the absolute limits of what's possible. PARICHAY has grown from a college gathering into a landmark event that draws participants from across institutions.

// What We Stand For
Our Mission

Innovate

PARICHAY is built on the belief that the best ideas come from collision — of disciplines, of minds, of approaches. We create the spark. You create the fire.

Compete

Five distinct arenas. Each one designed to push you past what you thought possible. Hack through the night, race a robot, win a debate — or do all three.

Connect

Meet mentors from the industry, collaborate with students you've never spoken to, and build relationships that outlast the leaderboard.

Celebrate

PARICHAY is more than competition. It's a celebration of every student who stayed up late learning something new and decided to show the world what they built.

// Edition History
The Journey

PARICHAY 1.0 — 2023

The beginning. A single-day fest with 3 events and 120 participants. The spark that started everything.

PARICHAY 2.0 — 2024

Expanded to 5 events, 280+ participants. First robotics event introduced. Prize pool doubled.

PARICHAY 3.0 — 2025

Inter-college participation opens up. 400+ students. Live gallery for Visual Odyssey. Industry mentors join hackathon judging.

PARICHAY 4.0 — 2026  ◈ YOU ARE HERE

The biggest edition yet. 500+ expected participants, 5 events, ₹50K+ prize pool, industry sponsors, and 24-hour hackathon format.
