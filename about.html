About The Fest

Four editions. One legacy. Infinite potential.

PARICHAY 4.0 is our college's premier annual technology and creative festival by the AIML department — a full-spectrum intellectual and competitive battleground where engineers, coders, designers and dreamers collide to push the absolute limits of what's possible. Held on 21st April 2026 at Seminar Hall 323 / Auditorium, T. John Institute of Technology, from 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM. PARICHAY has grown into a landmark event that draws participants from across institutions.

Chief Guest
[ CHIEF GUEST — PARICHAY 4.0 ]

Manohar Bangera

CMO, Vedicon Solutions Pvt Ltd
🌐 Technology Leader ✍ Author 🧘 Art of Living Teacher 🏃 Endurance Athlete

Manohar Bangera is a technology leader and writer with extensive international experience across global enterprises and high-growth startups. He has worked with organizations such as ABB, Cisco, and several emerging startups, contributing to complex software systems, product development, and engineering leadership initiatives.

A certified teacher with the Art of Living, Mano actively leads programs focused on mindfulness, stress management, and inner well-being — integrating technology and human development in his work and writing. He is the author of Arise. Play!, a modern fable about facing life challenges and winning.

An avid endurance enthusiast, Mano has trekked extensively in the Himalayas and is a committed long-distance runner, having completed multiple 10K marathon races — disciplines that echo in his professional philosophy of resilience and sustained effort.
