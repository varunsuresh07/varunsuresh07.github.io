// About the Event

THINK-INK EXPO is a creative Poster Presentation event designed to provide students with a platform to showcase their ideas, research, and innovations through visually engaging posters. The event encourages participants to think critically, present creatively, and communicate their concepts effectively while sharing knowledge on meaningful and trending topics.

// Presentation Format
01
Direct Presentation

All participating teams will present their posters directly in front of the panel of judges. No pre-screening round.

02
5–7 Minute Slot

Each team is allotted 5–7 minutes to present their poster, explain their concept, and walk the judges through their work.

03
Q&A Session

A brief Q&A session follows the presentation. Evaluation is done simultaneously by judges during your slot.

// Rules & Regulations
  • Each team must consist of 2–4 participants. Individual entries are not permitted.
  • Posters must be relevant to the selected theme. Off-theme entries will be penalised in scoring.
  • Content must be entirely original. Plagiarism of any kind is strictly prohibited and will lead to disqualification.
  • Participants must be able to clearly explain their poster to the judges. All team members may be questioned.
  • Use of charts, images, infographics, and diagrams is strongly encouraged to enhance visual impact.
  • Any form of misconduct, disruption, or unsportsmanlike behaviour will lead to immediate disqualification.
  • Judges' decision is final and binding. No appeals or disputes will be entertained post-evaluation.
