// About the Event

Think-Ink Expo is a celebration of intellect and expression at PARICHAY 4.0. This multifaceted creative event gathers writers, debaters, poets, and visual thinkers under one roof. Compete in Poster Making, Essay Writing, Extempore Speaking, or the Grand Debate. Each discipline is a stage to showcase your voice, vision, and values. No compiler required — your sharpest weapon is your mind.

// Rules & Regulations
  • Register for one sub-event only: Poster Making, Essay Writing, Extempore, or Grand Debate. Multi-category entries are void.
  • Poster Making — All materials provided. Create original artwork within 90 minutes. No digital devices or reference material permitted.
  • Essay Writing — Topic revealed on the day of the event. Duration: 60 minutes. Must be handwritten on provided answer sheets only.
  • Extempore — Topics drawn by lottery. 2 minutes preparation, 3 minutes delivery. No notes allowed at the podium under any circumstance.
  • Grand Debate — Teams of 2. Topic announced 48 hours prior. 5 minutes per side for arguments, 2 minutes rebuttal each.
  • Plagiarism or use of pre-prepared material in Essay/Extempore events leads to immediate disqualification and forfeiture of prize.
  • Criteria: Content Quality, Originality, Clarity, and Delivery. Judges' verdicts are final with no appeals accepted.
