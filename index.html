What Is Ignite?

IGNITE is our college's premier annual technology and creative festival — a 24-hour crucible where engineers, coders, designers, and dreamers collide to push the boundaries of what's possible.

From hackathons to robot wars, from pixel art to debate arenas — IGNITE 2026 is a full-spectrum intellectual and creative battleground. Compete, collaborate, and leave your mark on the most electric event of the academic year.

Expect mentors from industry, live judging, jaw-dropping prizes, and an atmosphere that buzzes with ambition and electricity.
