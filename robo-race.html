// About the Event

Robo Race at PARICHAY 4.0 challenges participants to design, build, and race autonomous or manually controlled robots through a brutal obstacle course. The arena features ramps, maze segments, speed gates, and narrow corridors that eliminate poorly engineered bots instantly. Teams must combine mechanical design, electronics, and embedded programming to build machines that are fast, durable, and smart. The crowd goes wild as circuits battle physics.

// Rules & Regulations
  • Robots must be fully built by the competing team. Pre-assembled commercial kits or externally fabricated components are not allowed.
  • Dimensions must not exceed 30×30×30 cm. Weight limit: 3 kg. All bots are measured and weighed during check-in before competing.
  • Autonomous bots must not use GPS. All navigation must be sensor-based: IR, ultrasonic, camera vision, or line-following sensors only.
  • Manual bots must use wired or wireless remote. Wireless bots must operate on approved ISM-band frequencies to prevent interference with other bots.
  • Race format: timed obstacle run with checkpoints. Best of 2 attempts counted. Bots that leave the track are repositioned at the last valid checkpoint.
  • Intentional interference with other bots — ramming, signal jamming, or physical blocking — results in immediate disqualification.
  • One 15-minute pit stop allowed per round for minor repairs only. Full structural rebuilds or major component swaps are not permitted mid-contest.
