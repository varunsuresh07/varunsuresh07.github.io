// About the Event

Robo Race is an exciting and innovation-driven event designed to challenge participants in building and controlling smart robotic cars. Organized by the Department of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, this competition brings together creativity, engineering skills, and problem-solving abilities.

In this event, we provide all the necessary materials required to build a robo car, ensuring a fair and hands-on experience for every team. Participants will design and assemble their own robot using the provided components.

Once the robot is built, teams must navigate it through a specially designed track. The race path includes obstacles and task-based challenges, where participants must demonstrate precision, control, and strategy to successfully complete the course.

// Requirements
  • Basic knowledge of robotics and circuits (recommended).
  • Team coordination and proper task division.
  • Ability to assemble and control the robot efficiently.
  • No additional materials or tools should be brought unless specified.
// Instructions
1
Collect Materials

Collect all materials from the organizers at the start of the event.

2
Assemble Your Robo Car

Assemble your robo car within the allotted time using only the provided components.

3
Test Your Robot

Test your robot before the race begins (if time is permitted). Place your robot at the starting point when instructed.

4
Navigate the Track

Navigate the robot through the track by avoiding obstacles and completing tasks.

5
Complete Within Time

Complete the track within the given time for better scoring. Participants must report 15 minutes before the event.

6
Follow Coordinator Instructions

Follow all instructions given by event coordinators. Practice rounds (if allowed) must be completed within the given time. Judges' decisions will be final.

// Rules & Regulations
  • Each team must consist of maximum 4 participants.
  • All materials will be provided by the organizers; participants are not allowed to use external components.
  • Teams must build the robo car within the given time limit.
  • The robot must operate only using the provided components.
  • Any kind of manual interference during the race is not allowed.
  • Teams must follow the instructions given by event coordinators at all times.
  • Damaging the provided materials will result in a penalty charge.
  • Judges' decisions will be final and binding.
